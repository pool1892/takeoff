# Make the procurement story understandable and testable

Read `AGENTS.md`, the shared demo-story issue, and the relevant UX/evaluation issue.
Keep the house-procurement ambition while using a small coherent material package.

The core story is: a task in the new buyer Ambiguous workspace wakes the Hermes
buyer on Christoph's computer; it discovers products and obtains/negotiates offers
from suppliers on another computer through website, email, and agent-to-agent
tooling; it asks the contractor a consequential question in Ambiguous; it publishes
the explained recommendation there. The supplier agent has a separate workspace.
Phone is fully planned stretch, with a complete core story if it is absent.

Partly scripted behavior is intentional. Author opportunities, a limited playbook,
and meaningful constraints. Decisions and the winning package should depend on the
observed information and approved requirements, not a predetermined savings result.
Try a changed stock/price/approval fact to check responsiveness.

Give the final recommendation evidence: selected product and supplier, quantity,
total payable, delivery, material tradeoffs, rejected alternatives, and the quote
or contractor answer that supports each important choice. Keep optional detail
inspectable without requiring the viewer to follow every message.

For comparison, validate complete plans before comparing costs. Record package
cost, human active time, elapsed time, and valid completions out of all attempts.
Reset the remote supplier world for each trial and keep commercial opportunities
equivalent. Let human participants negotiate too; record their experience and any
interface differences. Include failures and timeouts, not only a selected good run.

An initial-offer optimizer is not a human baseline. Secret supplier floors are not
automatically achievable buying prices. A few volunteers cannot establish broad
superiority over professional buyers. Report the actual participants, scenario,
trial count, validity, and observed tradeoffs. If no human participated, say that.

Ehsan owns narrative and production. Capture actual behavior, then use labeled
replay if useful for pacing. Simulated supplier businesses can communicate over real
channels; state those facts separately. Keep captions and claims consistent with
the selected recorded run and the complete comparison results. Drafts and prepared
assets are not submitted/published outcomes unless that action was performed.
